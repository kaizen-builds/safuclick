      Limits, plainly

      It flags known patterns and never says “safe”. A new trick can slip through until the loop learns it. Some scam sites cloak and show bots a blank page; we caught aave-fi.info doing exactly that, and the cloaking lab shows when that makes a check inconclusive. Wallet evidence (permits, batches, approvals) needs the gym’s mock wallet, so it can’t be seen on real sites. Address poisoning, malware and hijacked real domains are out of scope. Live checks are capped because each one is a real Kane run.
